Privacy Policy

What Tokenomics touches, and what it doesn't.

Short version: Tokenomics shows your AI usage using the sign-ins you already have. We never receive it. The Mac app, the browser extension, and Tokenomics for Safari all talk only to the AI services you use and to your own Mac. No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking inside any of them. The marketing website you're reading uses Cloudflare's cookie-free Web Analytics and counts anonymous download requests; details below.

Effective date: November 25, 2026 Owned by: Acorn Designs, LLC Created by: Rob Stout Contact: hello@trytokenomics.com

What this policy covers

This policy is published at https://trytokenomics.com/privacy. It covers everything called Tokenomics:

  • The Tokenomics Mac app (menu bar app and desktop widgets), downloaded from this website, GitHub, or Homebrew.
  • The Tokenomics browser extension for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge (and other Chromium browsers that can install from those stores).
  • Tokenomics for Safari, a Mac App Store app that contains a Safari extension.
  • This website, trytokenomics.com.

The key point is the same for all of them: Tokenomics does not send your data to the author or to any server the author controls. The app, the extension, and the Safari app have no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no accounts. They connect only to the AI services you already use (to ask for your own usage numbers) and, on your computer, to each other.

Tokenomics is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any AI service it works with. Names such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney, ElevenLabs, Grok, Perplexity, and Leonardo are trademarks of their owners and are used only to say which services Tokenomics works with.

Mac app: what it accesses

Tokenomics reads authentication credentials stored locally on your Mac by the AI tools you have installed. It never asks you to paste credentials it could have read automatically; it only asks for an API key for providers that don't have a CLI.

  • Anthropic (Claude Code, whose limits Claude chats share) — OAuth token from macOS Keychain (Claude Code-credentials)
  • OpenAI (Codex CLI) — usage from Codex's local session files, and its sign-in in ~/.codex/auth.json
  • Google (Gemini CLI) — usage counted from Gemini CLI's local session files, and its sign-in in ~/.gemini/oauth_creds.json
  • GitHub Copilot — OAuth token via the gh CLI
  • Cursor — session token from Cursor's local config
  • Stability AI, Runway — API keys you paste into Tokenomics, stored in the macOS Keychain (plus an ElevenLabs API key, if you added one in an earlier version; current versions get ElevenLabs from the browser extension)

These credentials are read into memory as needed. The Mac app does not copy them anywhere else, log them, or send them anywhere other than the provider endpoints below.

Besides its own settings and the usage numbers its widgets need, the Mac app adds a few things to your Mac:

  • Small link files in the Native Messaging folders of Chromium browsers you have (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, Arc), so the browser extension can hand usage numbers to the Mac app. They contain only the helper's location and the extension's ID.
  • If you use guided setup to install a coding tool: the tool itself (Codex CLI and Gemini CLI in ~/.tokenomics-cli, Claude Code in ~/.local/bin), Homebrew, Node.js or the GitHub CLI when that tool needs them, and one line in ~/.zprofile (or ~/.bash_profile) so your Terminal can find Claude Code.

Uninstall Tokenomics… in Settings removes the app's settings, saved numbers, link files and the Keychain items it created. It leaves your AI tools, and Claude Code's own sign-in, in place.

Like any internet request, the services Tokenomics contacts (the AI providers above, and GitHub for update checks) can see your IP address when your Mac connects to them. Tokenomics does not add anything to that beyond what the request itself needs.

Browser extension (Chrome and Edge)

The Tokenomics extension shows your usage for AI services you're signed in to, in a toolbar popup and a small badge. It runs in your browser. It does not send anything to the author. Its only network requests go to the AI services listed below, to ask for your own account's usage.

Nothing happens until you agree. When you install the extension, it shows a short consent screen (in a settings tab and in the popup) that explains what it reads and stores. Until you click Agree and turn on, it requests no usage, saves no tokens, logs no messages, and sends nothing to the Mac app. If you click No thanks, it stays off.

How it gets your usage

You must already be signed in to each AI service in your browser. The extension then uses your existing sign-in, the same way the service's own website does, to ask that service how much of your plan you've used. It does not ask for your password and has no account of its own.

Service What the extension requests (your account only) Sign-in it uses
Claude (claude.ai) claude.ai/api/organizations and /organizations/<id>/usage Your browser's existing session cookie
ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) /api/auth/session, /backend-api/me, /backend-api/wham/usage Your session cookie, and a short-lived access token that chatgpt.com returns to you (held in memory for the request, not saved)
Midjourney www.midjourney.com/api/user-account Your session cookie

What the extension stores, and where

The extension keeps the following in your browser's local extension storage on your computer. It is not synced to your Google or Microsoft account by the extension, and it is not sent to the author.

  • Usage readings: percentages, reset times, and plan name for each service.
  • Settings: which service is pinned, which tab you last viewed, which services are shown, whether to show site names instead of logos, and the ChatGPT plan you chose (if you override it).
  • A ChatGPT message log: used only as a backup estimate when ChatGPT's exact numbers are unavailable. Each entry is just the time and the model name of a message you sent on chatgpt.com. It never contains your message text. Entries older than 7 days are deleted.
  • Saved sign-in tokens and API keys: the ElevenLabs access token and the Leonardo access token and account ID, plus an ElevenLabs or Leonardo API key if you choose to paste one in settings. They're saved so the extension can keep refreshing your usage without you reopening those sites. Each is used only to call the service that issued it. The extension does not add its own encryption to this storage, so anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile on your computer could read them. Tokens expire on the service's own schedule; an API key works until you remove it in settings or revoke it on the service's site. (The Suno token is never saved.)
  • Learned renewal dates: for Leonardo and Suno, which don't report when your monthly credits renew, the last balance seen and the date a refill was first noticed, so the extension can estimate your next renewal date.
  • Your consent: that you agreed to the consent screen, which version of it, and when.
  • Connection status: the last 50 status messages about the link to the Mac app, and rate-limit backoff timers.

The extension never saves your passwords, the text of your conversations, or your cookies. (It uses your cookies by letting the browser attach them to its requests, as it would for the site itself.)

What the extension watches on the AI sites

On a few sites, the extension runs a small script inside the page so it can read your usage. Specifically:

  • chatgpt.com: it notices each time you send a message and records only the time and model name (for the backup estimate above).
  • gemini.google.com: it asks Gemini for your usage using the page's own session, and passes the resulting usage numbers to the extension.
  • elevenlabs.io: it reads the access token that ElevenLabs' sign-in stores in your browser, and passes it to the extension.
  • app.leonardo.ai: it watches the page's own requests to Leonardo's servers to pick up the access token and account ID, then passes them to the extension. It does not change, block, or delay those requests.
  • suno.com: it reads suno.com's short-lived sign-in token from the page's cookie, uses it once to ask Suno for your credit balance, and passes only the resulting numbers to the extension.

The extension does not read your prompts, replies, files, or browsing history, and it does nothing on any site not listed on this page.

Link to the Mac app (optional)

If the Tokenomics Mac app is installed, the extension uses your browser's Native Messaging to talk to a small helper that ships with the Mac app. This stays on your computer. Over this link, the extension sends your usage readings (percentages, reset times, plan name) and which services you've chosen to show, and receives the Mac app's readings and settings back. Sign-in tokens, cookies, and the ChatGPT message log are never sent over this link. ElevenLabs works the same way: only the usage numbers cross, never the token or API key. If you've also given the Mac app its own ElevenLabs API key, the Mac app uses its own reading instead. If the Mac app isn't installed, the extension works on its own and this link simply does nothing.

Permissions the extension asks for, and why

  • Storage: to save the items listed above.
  • Alarms: to refresh your usage every few minutes while your browser is open.
  • Native Messaging: to talk to the Mac app, as described above.
  • Access to the AI sites listed above (claude.ai, chatgpt.com, www.midjourney.com, gemini.google.com, elevenlabs.io and api.us.elevenlabs.io, grok.com, www.perplexity.ai, app.leonardo.ai, api.leonardo.ai and cloud.leonardo.ai, suno.com, www.udio.com): to request your usage and run the page scripts described above. The extension has no access to any other website.

Your controls

  • "Stop reading and delete my data" in the extension's settings turns it off and deletes everything it stored, including the saved tokens and API keys. It stays off until you agree again.
  • Uninstalling the extension also deletes everything it stored.
  • Signing out of an AI service makes its saved token stop working, so the extension can no longer read that service.
  • Browser settings let you restrict the extension's site access at any time.

Limited use

Tokenomics uses the data it handles only to show you your own usage and to keep the Mac app and extension in sync. It does not sell or transfer user data to anyone, does not use or transfer it for anything unrelated to that purpose, and does not use or transfer it to determine creditworthiness or for lending. Nobody, including the author, reads your data.

Tokenomics for Safari

Tokenomics for Safari is a sandboxed Mac App Store app that contains a Safari extension. The Safari extension works like the browser extension described above: it uses your existing sign-in on the supported AI sites to request your own usage, and it keeps the same items in Safari's extension storage on your Mac. It sends nothing to the author.

If the Tokenomics Mac app is installed, the Safari extension shares your usage readings and your show/hide settings with it through a shared folder on your Mac (an App Group container). That data never leaves your computer. The Safari app itself has no network access of its own, has no accounts, and collects no data. Its only outbound link is a button that opens trytokenomics.com in your browser.

The App Store privacy label for Tokenomics for Safari reads "Data Not Collected", because nothing is sent to the author or any third party.

About this website

The Tokenomics app and the marketing website at trytokenomics.com are separate things, and they have different (but both narrow) data footprints. Everything above this line is about the apps and the extension. Everything in this section is about the website.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare provides standard server-side request analytics — visit counts, top pages, country breakdown — drawn from request headers as the page is served.

The site also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, so we can see which pages help people and how fast they load. Cloudflare adds a small script to each page (loaded from static.cloudflareinsights.com) that reports the page you viewed, the page that linked you here, your browser and device type, your country, and how long the page took to load. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and doesn't follow you across other sites. We see only totals, never individual visitors. There are no other third-party scripts, trackers or ads on the site, and no fingerprinting.

The site's fonts are served from trytokenomics.com itself, so loading a page doesn't contact Google or any other third party.

We also count download requests so we can estimate how many people are using Tokenomics. When you click a download button (or run brew install rob-stout/tap/tokenomics), the request goes through trytokenomics.com/download/..., which records a single row before redirecting you to the actual .dmg file on GitHub. The row contains:

  • Timestamp (UTC)
  • Version requested (e.g. 2.8.7)
  • Channel — web, brew, or other (a coarse bucket from the request's User-Agent; the User-Agent string itself is not stored)
  • Country code (ISO-2, from Cloudflare's CF-IPCountry header)

What we do not store: IP addresses, User-Agent strings, cookies, referrers, anything that could identify you. The download counter is one anonymous row per download. Aggregates live in a Cloudflare D1 database that only the author can query.

The browser-extension and Safari apps do not use this website for anything except opening it when you click a link, and they do not send it any data. Installing from the Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons, or the Mac App Store does not touch the download counter. Those stores keep their own install statistics under their own privacy policies.

Download-counter rows contain nothing that identifies you, and they are kept for as long as the site runs.

What Tokenomics does NOT do

  • No analytics or telemetry of any kind from inside the Mac app, the browser extension, or Tokenomics for Safari
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code in the Mac app, the browser extension or Tokenomics for Safari. (The Mac app uses the Sparkle update framework; the extension bundles the open-source Preact and webextension-polyfill libraries. None of them send data to anyone.)
  • No data collection, aggregation, or transmission to any server controlled by the author
  • No tracking of how you use the apps or extension
  • No crash reporting
  • No ads, no accounts, and no selling or sharing of data

Data retention

Mac app: holds your credentials in memory while it runs and discards them when you quit. It does save usage readings (percentages and reset times, not credentials) to a shared folder on your Mac so the desktop widgets can display them, and it saves your settings. Uninstalling the app removes these.

Browser extension and Tokenomics for Safari: keep their data in the browser's local extension storage until you click "Stop reading and delete my data" in settings or uninstall the extension. Either one deletes it. See "What the extension stores" above for exactly what's kept and for how long (the ChatGPT message log is trimmed to 7 days).

Website: keeps anonymous download-counter rows for as long as the site runs. Cloudflare keeps the Web Analytics totals under its own retention policy. See "About this website."

Third-party services

When Tokenomics asks an AI service for your usage, that request goes to the service and is handled under that service's own terms and privacy policy. Tokenomics does not control those services or what they do with the request. Tokenomics is independent software and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of them. Using Tokenomics with a service may be subject to that service's terms; please read them.

Your rights and the author's role

Because the author does not receive your data, there is nothing for the author to give you a copy of, correct, or delete, other than the anonymous download-counter rows, which contain no information that identifies you. Whatever you want to know or change about your account data is with the AI service that holds it. If you think we hold something about you, email hello@trytokenomics.com and we'll look. This applies the same way wherever you live, including California and the EU/UK.

Children
