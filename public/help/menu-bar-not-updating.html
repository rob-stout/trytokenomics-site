Help / Fixing problems

Why did my menu bar numbers stop updating?

When you haven't used Claude Code for a while, Tokenomics pauses checking until you're active again or click refresh. The next update (2.10.5) keeps checking in the background instead.

Tokenomics checks each tool on its own schedule, from every minute (Codex CLI and Gemini CLI, which it reads from files on your Mac) to every 10 minutes (Stability AI). To go easy on each service, it pauses checking when you haven't used Claude Code for about 9 minutes, and picks back up as soon as you're active again.

  • The refresh button (or ⌘R) checks every tool right away.
  • The line at the bottom of the popover says when the numbers were last updated, like “Updated 2m ago”.

Coming in the next update (2.10.5): instead of pausing, Tokenomics keeps checking at least every 15 minutes while you're idle, and opening the popover refreshes anything that's out of date. Tokenomics will offer it when it's out (how updates work).

Still stuck on old numbers? A service may be rate-limiting Tokenomics (what that looks like), or a sign-in may have expired (how to fix it). Web tools also need your browser open; see how the Mac app and extension work together.
