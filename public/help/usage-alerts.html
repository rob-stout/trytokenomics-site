How do usage alerts work, and how do I change them?

Tokenomics notifies you when a tool crosses 80% by default, and again if you hit the limit. Change the threshold, the window or turn alerts off in Settings → Notifications.

Each tool alerts you once when its usage crosses a threshold, so you can slow down before you hit a wall.

  • The warning reads like “Claude 5-Hour Window at 80%”, then “You may hit your limit soon” and when it resets.
  • If you keep going and reach 100%, you get one more: “You've hit your usage limit.”
  • When a limit you hit resets, Tokenomics tells you it's “ready again”.

Click an alert to open that tool's tab.

Changing them

Open Settings (the gear in the popover) → Notifications:

  • Per tool: switch alerts on or off, and set the threshold from 50% to 100% in steps of 10. The default is on, at 80%.
  • Alert Window: choose whether the Short window (like Claude's 5-hour), the Long one (like the 7-day), or Both can trigger alerts. The default is Short.

After an alert, that tool won't alert again until its usage drops 10 points below your threshold, usually when the window resets.

Not getting any alerts?

macOS asks for permission the first time an alert is due, not during setup. If you said no, Settings → Notifications shows “Notifications are disabled in System Settings.” with a link to turn them back on.

Alerts come from the Mac app. The browser extension doesn't send notifications.
