Why do ChatGPT and Gemini show two meters?

ChatGPT and Codex CLI share an OpenAI login but have separate limits, and so do the Gemini app and Gemini CLI. Tokenomics shows each limit on its own.

ChatGPT and Codex CLI are two different products that share one OpenAI login but count against separate limits. ChatGPT has its own chat limits; Codex CLI has its own weekly quota tied to your plan. Same account, different meters, and OpenAI doesn't give a combined number, so Tokenomics shows both and you can see which one you're closer to.

This is changing in December 2026: OpenAI is moving Plus, Pro and Business plans to one allowance shared across ChatGPT and Codex. See how ChatGPT's limits work and how Codex's limits work.

The same is true for Gemini: the Gemini app at gemini.google.com and the Gemini CLI share a Google account but track usage separately. See how the Gemini app's limits work.

Claude is the exception: chats and Claude Code share one set of limits. See how Claude's limits work.

In widgets

Widgets show each limit as its own row, because there's no room to nest them. If you've connected both ChatGPT and Codex CLI, you'll see two OpenAI rows. On the small widget you pick exactly one: open the widget's edit panel and choose ChatGPT or Codex CLI.

Upgrading from an earlier version? If your small widget was set to “OpenAI” or “Google AI,” it now shows Codex CLI or Gemini CLI, because that's what those entries were already tracking. To show the chat limit instead, pick it in the edit panel.
