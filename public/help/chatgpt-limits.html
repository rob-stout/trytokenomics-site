Help / How AI limits work

How do ChatGPT's usage limits work?

Everyday chat is unlimited on most plans, but Thinking, Pro models and tools like images and voice have their own limits. From December, Plus, Pro and Business share one allowance across ChatGPT and Codex.

The limits

  • Everyday chat: unlimited on Free and Go, subject to abuse safeguards.
  • Tools: file uploads, image generation, voice and data analysis have separate limits, even on plans with unlimited chat.
  • Thinking and Pro models: have their own caps. On Pro, the top model has a weekly limit. OpenAI no longer publishes message counts for these.

When you reach a Thinking or Pro-model limit, ChatGPT often switches you to another model instead of stopping, so it's easy to miss.

By plan

  • Free and Go ($8/month): unlimited everyday chat; tools are limited. Go may include ads.
  • Plus ($20/month): may have message caps, especially when demand is high.
  • Pro ($100, $200 or $500/month): three tiers. “Unlimited” access is subject to abuse guardrails, and the top models have weekly limits.
  • Business: seats use their included limits first, then the workspace's credit pool.

ChatGPT and Codex: one allowance from December

Today, Codex has its own allowance, separate from chat. It's already shared with ChatGPT Work, ChatGPT for Excel and Workspace Agents. Starting in December 2026, Plus, Pro and Business plans share one allowance across ChatGPT and Codex, measured in tokens. Quick “Instant” chat replies don't count against it. Some people may see the change early. How Codex limits work.

When you hit a limit

Wait for the reset, switch models, upgrade, or (for the shared allowance) buy credits.

Where to check

Settings → Usage in ChatGPT shows remaining usage and reset times where OpenAI provides them.
