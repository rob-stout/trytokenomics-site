Help / Your numbers

Are the numbers exact?

Almost always. Tokenomics shows the limits each tool reports. The few readings it has to work out itself are marked as estimates.

Almost always. For nearly every tool, Tokenomics shows the same limits and reset times the tool itself uses.

A few readings have to be worked out:

  • ChatGPT: the extension normally reads ChatGPT's real limits. If it can't, it falls back to counting the messages you send from that browser (the time and model only, never what you wrote) against your plan's limits. See fixing the ChatGPT plan.
  • Gemini CLI: Google doesn't report it, so Tokenomics counts your requests from the session files Gemini CLI keeps on your Mac, against the daily limits of the plan you choose. See how Gemini CLI is counted.

Estimated readings are labelled: the extension shows an estimated tag, and the Mac app's popover says “Estimated from…” under the numbers.

If a number looks wrong, click refresh first. If it's still off, tell us which tool and plan you're on.
