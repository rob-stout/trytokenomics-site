How do I check my GitHub Copilot premium requests?

See premium requests in your GitHub Copilot settings, your editor, or your Mac menu bar with Tokenomics. The allowance resets on the 1st of each month (UTC).

  • On GitHub: your Copilot settings page on github.com shows premium requests used this month. The billing pages break it down further.
  • In your editor: Copilot's status menu in supported editors shows your usage too.
  • In your menu bar, with Tokenomics: from the next update (2.10.5), paid plans get a Premium Requests bar, like “75 / 300 used”, with a pace dot so you can tell if you'll run out before the month ends. Free plans get Chat and Completions bars instead. What the Copilot meters show.

Premium request allowances reset on the 1st of each month at midnight UTC.

Tokenomics reads Copilot through the GitHub CLI's sign-in. If it isn't connected yet, see the setup guide.
