How do Suno credits work?

Free gets 50 credits a day. Pro and Premier get a monthly allowance (2,500 or 10,000) that resets on your subscription date, with no carryover. Paid users who run out get 50 a day until renewal.

By plan

  • Free: 50 credits a day, resetting daily (UTC).
  • Pro ($10/month): 2,500 credits a month, 20 downloads.
  • Premier ($30/month): 10,000 credits, 60 downloads.
  • Platinum ($120/month): 15,000 song credits plus separate visual credits, 120 downloads.

Reset and top-ups

  • Paid credits reset monthly on the day and time you subscribed, on yearly plans too. Unused credits don't carry over.
  • If a paid plan runs out early, you get 50 free credits a day until renewal.
  • Bought top-up credits never expire, but need an active subscription.

Where to check

suno.com/account.

In Tokenomics

The browser extension shows credits left against your monthly allowance. Suno doesn't report a renewal date, so Tokenomics learns it the first time your credits refill. More on renewal dates.
