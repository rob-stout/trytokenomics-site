Help / How AI limits work

How do Midjourney's Fast Hours work?

Midjourney limits Fast GPU time, not images. Each plan gets Fast hours every month (3.3 on Basic up to 60 on Mega); Relax mode is unlimited on Standard and up and doesn't use them.

What's limited

Midjourney counts GPU time while it's generating, not clock time or images. Roughly: an image prompt takes about a minute of Fast time, an upscale about 2 minutes, and a batch of four videos 8 minutes or more. Higher quality settings use 2× or 4×.

Fast hours by plan (per month)

  • Basic ($10): 3.3 hours (200 minutes). No Relax mode.
  • Standard ($30): 15 hours, plus unlimited Relax for images.
  • Pro ($60): 30 hours, plus unlimited Relax for images and SD video. Stealth mode.
  • Mega ($120): 60 hours, same extras as Pro.

The three speeds

  • Fast: uses your Fast hours.
  • Relax: unlimited and free of Fast time, but queued (up to about 30 minutes, longer if you use it a lot). Not available on Basic.
  • Turbo: up to 4× faster, at 2× the Fast time.

Reset and extra time

Fast time resets when your subscription renews. Unused time doesn't carry over, on monthly and yearly plans alike. Extra Fast time is $4 an hour and expires after 60 days; free time earned by rating images expires after 30.

Where to check

Manage Subscription → Usage Details, or /info in Discord.
