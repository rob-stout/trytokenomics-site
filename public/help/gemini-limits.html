Help / How AI limits work

How do the Gemini app's usage limits work?

The Gemini app's limit refreshes every 5 hours until you reach a weekly cap. It's based on compute, so images, video, Deep Research and the Pro model use it faster.

How it works

Since May 2026, the Gemini app measures compute rather than counting prompts. Your limit refreshes every 5 hours until you reach your weekly limit. Google doesn't publish the numbers for either.

What a request costs depends on how complex it is, the features it uses and how long the chat has run. Image, video and music generation, Deep Research, the Pro model, extended thinking and Deep Think use it faster; video costs the most. A single complex prompt can take a noticeable bite out of a 5-hour block.

By plan

  • Free: the standard limit. Uses Auto model routing by default.
  • Google AI Plus: 2× standard.
  • Google AI Pro: 4× standard.
  • Google AI Ultra: 5× or 20× the AI Pro limit, depending on the subscription.

When you hit the limit

Keep chatting with the lighter Flash-Lite model, wait for the refresh, upgrade, or (AI Pro and Ultra) buy AI credits at API prices.

What's separate

The Gemini API and AI Studio have their own per-minute and per-day limits, per project. Work and school accounts follow their own rules. Gemini CLI is separate again.

Where to check

In gemini.google.com, open Settings → Usage Limits.

In Tokenomics

The browser extension reads Google's own usage numbers and shows the 5-hour window and the weekly window, each with its real reset time. Google doesn't report your plan, so you pick it once. Why there are two meters.

Checked against each provider's own pages in November 2026. Providers change limits often, so their usage page is the final word.
