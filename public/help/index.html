    Getting set up

    • Which AI tools does Tokenomics work with?

      From Claude Code and Cursor to ChatGPT, Midjourney and Suno. Here's the full list, what each meter tracks, and how each one connects.

    • Which browsers does the extension work in?

      Google Chrome, plus Brave, Arc, Vivaldi and Opera from the Chrome Web Store. Versions for Microsoft Edge and Safari are on the way.

    • Does the extension work without the Mac app?

      Yes. It works on its own in your browser. The Mac app adds menu bar rings, widgets and coding tools, and the two stay in sync.

    • How do the Mac app and the browser extension work together?

      The Mac app reads your coding tools, the extension reads your web tools, and they share usage numbers on your computer so each shows the full picture.

    • Does Tokenomics work on Windows or Linux?

      The browser extension does, in Chrome. The menu bar app, widgets and coding-tool tracking need a Mac for now.

    • Is Tokenomics free?

      Yes. The Mac app and the browser extension are free, with no subscription, no ads and no account to make.

    • Does the extension start reading as soon as I install it?

      No. It shows a short consent screen first, and nothing is read until you click Agree and turn on.

    • How does signing in work?

      There's nothing to sign in to. The Mac app reads the sign-in your coding tools already keep, the extension uses the sites you're signed in to, and Stability AI and Runway take an API key.

    • How do I set up ElevenLabs?

      Visit elevenlabs.io with the extension installed, or paste an ElevenLabs API key into the extension. The Mac app shows whatever the extension reads.

    • How do I set up Leonardo?

      Visit app.leonardo.ai with the extension installed and it picks up your sign-in, or paste a Leonardo API key in the extension's settings.

    • What does guided setup install on my Mac?

      Only what each coding tool needs: Homebrew, Node.js, the tool itself, and for Claude Code a line that adds it to your PATH. Terminal opens for installs and sign-ins.

    • I use Safari. How do I track my web tools?

      Tokenomics for Safari is in App Store review. Until it's out, use the extension in Chrome, Brave, Arc or another Chromium browser alongside Safari.

    Using Tokenomics

    • How do I uninstall Tokenomics?

      Use Settings → Uninstall Tokenomics… to remove the app and everything it set up. To remove the extension, right-click its toolbar icon and choose Remove.

    • How do I choose what the menu bar shows?

      By default the menu bar shows whichever tool is closest to its limit. You can pin one tool instead from the popover's Menu Bar Display menu.

    • What does the pace dot mean?

      The pace dot shows where your usage would be if you'd used your limit evenly since it last reset. If your fill is past the dot, you're on track to run out early.

    • How do usage alerts work, and how do I change them?

      Tokenomics notifies you when a tool crosses 80% by default, and again if you hit the limit. Change the threshold, the window or turn alerts off in Settings → Notifications.

    • How do I add and set up the desktop widgets?

      Right-click your desktop, choose Edit Widgets and search for Tokenomics. Small shows one tool; medium, large and extra large show more.

    • How do I add, hide, reorder or remove a tool?

      Add tools from Settings → Setup providers…, hide them with their switch in AI Connections, and reorder tabs by holding ⌘ and dragging.

    • What does Group by Company do?

      It puts each company's products under one tab, like ChatGPT and Codex CLI under OpenAI, with a section for each inside. It's on by default.

    • How do updates work?

      The Mac app checks for updates automatically and tells you when one is ready; you choose when to install it. The extension updates itself through the Chrome Web Store.

    • Does Tokenomics start when I log in?

      Only if you turn on Launch at Login in Settings. Otherwise it's gone from the menu bar after a restart until you open it again.

    • Keyboard shortcuts, Siri and text size

      ⌘R refreshes, ⌘-drag reorders tabs, and Siri and Shortcuts can check or refresh your usage. Text size is in Settings.

    • What's the number on the extension's toolbar icon?

      It's the short-window usage of whichever site is closest to its limit, or the one you pin. Change it from the popup's Toolbar Display menu.

    Your numbers

    • How do Claude's 5-hour and 7-day limits work?

      The 5-hour window is the rolling cap most people hit first; the 7-day window is the weekly ceiling. Chats and Claude Code share both.

    • Why do ChatGPT and Gemini show two meters?

      ChatGPT and Codex CLI share an OpenAI login but have separate limits, and so do the Gemini app and Gemini CLI. Tokenomics shows each limit on its own.

    • Are the numbers exact?

      Almost always. Tokenomics shows the limits each tool reports. The few readings it has to work out itself are marked as estimates.

    • What are Midjourney's Fast Hours?

      Fast Hours are the GPU time Midjourney gives your plan each month for Fast mode. Tokenomics shows how many you've used. Relax mode doesn't count.

    • What are Extra Usage and Credits Balance?

      Extra Usage shows how much of your monthly extra-usage spending limit you've used on Claude. Credits Balance shows prepaid credits on your OpenAI account.

    • ChatGPT shows the wrong plan or limit. Can I fix it?

      Usually Tokenomics reads ChatGPT's real limits. If it can't, it counts your messages against your plan's limits, and you can set the plan yourself in the extension's settings.

    • Why don't Leonardo or Suno show a renewal date yet?

      Those services report your balance but not when it renews. Tokenomics learns your renewal day the first time it sees your balance refill.

    • How are Gemini CLI numbers counted, and which plan should I pick?

      Google doesn't report Gemini CLI usage, so Tokenomics counts today's requests from Gemini CLI's own files on your Mac, against the daily limits of the plan you choose.

    • What do the GitHub Copilot meters show?

      Paid Copilot plans show your premium requests used this month. Free plans show your monthly chat and code-completion allowances.

    • Why don't my Codex CLI numbers change, or say “No active session”?

      Codex CLI's numbers come from the logs Codex writes on your Mac, so they only change after you use Codex. ChatGPT in the browser doesn't move them.

    Fixing problems

    • Something's not working. What can I try?

      Most problems clear up by checking you're signed in, clicking refresh, or reopening your browser. Here's the full checklist.

    • Why did my menu bar numbers stop updating?

      Tokenomics checks less often when you're not actively coding, and catches up when you open the popover. Older versions could stop checking entirely; update to 2.10.5.

    • What does “Couldn't load usage data — check back in a few minutes” mean?

      The service asked Tokenomics to slow down. It keeps showing your last numbers and tries again after a short wait. Here's that and the other error messages.

    • Why does macOS ask for my password to read my Claude Code sign-in?

      Claude Code keeps its sign-in in your Mac's Keychain. Click Allow Access, enter your Mac password, and choose Always Allow so macOS stops asking.

    • It says “authentication expired” or “Session expired”. How do I fix it?

      Your coding tool's sign-in ran out. Sign in to the tool again, or click Reconnect to be walked through it.

    • Guided setup showed an error. What do I do?

      What each guided-setup message means and how to get past it: Terminal permission, Homebrew prompts, sign-in not detected, and more.

    • Why does my widget show old numbers or “Open Tokenomics to sync”?

      Widgets show what the Mac app last read. If the app isn't running, they can't update. Tap the widget's refresh button to open the app and refresh.

    • How do I report a bug or send feedback?

      Open Settings → Report Bugs / Feedback, or email hello@trytokenomics.com. Include the tool, your plan and your Tokenomics version.

    Privacy & permissions

    • Is my data sent anywhere? Does Tokenomics see my chats?

      No. Nothing goes to any server the author controls: no analytics, telemetry or tracking. The extension reads usage numbers, never your chats or prompts.

    • What can the browser extension access, and why?

      Only the AI sites it works with, to read your own usage numbers. It doesn't ask for access to all websites. Here's what each permission is for.

    • Is Tokenomics affiliated with these AI services?

      No. Tokenomics is independent and unofficial. It reads your own usage with your own sign-in.
