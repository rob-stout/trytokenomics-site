How do I add and set up the desktop widgets?

Right-click your desktop, choose Edit Widgets and search for Tokenomics. Small shows one tool; medium, large and extra large show more.

  1. Right-click your desktop and choose Edit Widgets.
  2. Search for Tokenomics and pick the AI Usage widget.
  3. Choose a size and drag it where you want it.

The sizes

  • Small: one tool as two rings. By default it shows whichever tool is closest to its limit. To pick a specific one, right-click the widget, choose Edit “AI Usage”, and set Provider.
  • Medium: up to 3 tools as bars.
  • Large: up to 7.
  • Extra large (macOS 27 and later): up to 14.

If you have more tools than fit, the widget says “+3 in app” (or however many). Widgets follow the tab order you set in the popover. On the small widget, the inner ring is the short window and the outer ring is the long one, the same as the menu bar.

Widgets show what the Mac app last read. If they look out of date, see why a widget shows old numbers.
