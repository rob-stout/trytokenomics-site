How do Runway credits work?

Runway charges credits per second of video (Gen-4.5 is 12 a second). Plans include 625 to 9,500 credits a month. Unlimited plans move to Max on November 30, 2026, and API credits are separate.

By plan (credits per month)

  • Free: 125 credits, once, never expire.
  • Standard ($15): 625 (about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5).
  • Pro ($35): 2,250 (about 3 minutes).
  • Max ($95): 9,500 (about 13 minutes).
  • Team: 6,900 per seat, pooled.

Video is charged per second: Gen-4.5 costs 12 credits a second, so a 5-second clip is 60 credits.

Reset, rollover and purchases

  • Plan credits refresh on your billing date.
  • Standard and Pro: unused credits expire. Max and Team: up to a month rolls over.
  • Bought credits never expire, and are used after plan and rollover credits.

Unlimited and Explore Mode are ending

Unlimited closed to new sign-ups in June 2026. Existing Unlimited plans move to Max on November 30, 2026, and Max has no Explore Mode.

App credits vs API credits

Credits in the Runway app and credits for Runway's developer API are completely separate. API credits are bought per project, cost $0.01 each, and don't reset monthly.
