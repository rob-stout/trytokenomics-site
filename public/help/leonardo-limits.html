Help / How AI limits work

How do Leonardo's tokens work?

Free gets 150 tokens a day. Paid plans get a monthly allowance (8,500 to 60,000) with a rollover bank of up to three months, and unlimited relaxed generation on Premium and up once tokens run out.

By plan

  • Free: 150 tokens a day. Resets every 24 hours; unused tokens expire.
  • Essential ($12/month): 8,500 a month.
  • Premium ($30): 25,000 a month, plus unlimited relaxed image generation on selected models.
  • Ultimate ($60): 60,000 a month, plus unlimited relaxed video on selected models.

What a generation costs depends on the model, feature, size and number of images; Leonardo doesn't publish a fixed price per image.

Reset, rollover and top-ups

  • Paid plans reset monthly on your subscription start date (upgrading doesn't move it).
  • Unused tokens go into a rollover bank of up to three months' worth, used only after this month's tokens. Cancelling clears it.
  • Top-up tokens never expire, but need an active subscription.
  • “Unlimited” relaxed generation starts only after your tokens run out, and it's slower.

Where to check

The token counter in Leonardo. Hover it to see fast tokens, the rollover bank and top-ups.
