Keyboard shortcuts, Siri and text size

⌘R refreshes, ⌘-drag reorders tabs, and Siri and Shortcuts can check or refresh your usage. Text size is in Settings.

Keyboard shortcuts in the popover

  • ⌘R refreshes every tool.
  • ⌘-drag a tab to reorder it.
  • ⌘Q quits Tokenomics.

Siri, Spotlight and Shortcuts

Tokenomics adds three actions you can use in the Shortcuts app, Spotlight or with Siri:

  • Check Usage, for example “Check Tokenomics usage”.
  • Refresh Usage: “Refresh Tokenomics”.
  • Show Usage: “Show Tokenomics”.

Text size

Settings → Text Size offers Compact, Medium and Large. The popover resizes to fit.
