How do Grok's usage limits work?

Paid Grok plans share one weekly usage pool across chat, voice, images, video and Cursor. The free tier has its own allowance on a separate schedule.

How it works

Since June 2026, paid Grok plans have one shared weekly usage pool instead of separate limits for each feature. Chat, voice, image and video generation all draw from it, at different rates depending on the compute they need. It's shared across the Grok apps, the Grok tab on X and Cursor.

The pool resets every week on a schedule shown in Settings → Usage.

Guides that quote “N queries every 2 hours” or separate Think and DeepSearch caps describe the old system.

Plans

New customers get The X Subscription (since October 2026): Plus ($8), Premium ($30, 4× Plus), Super ($100, 15× Plus), Ultra ($200, 50× Plus). Older SuperGrok and X Premium plans continue unless you switch. xAI publishes only multiples, not actual amounts.

The free tier isn't part of the weekly pool. Free chat and voice have their own allowance that resets on its own schedule.

When you hit the limit

Paid features pause until the weekly reset, but you can buy Extra Usage Credits (web only, from $5, priced higher per action than included usage, expire after a year), turn on Auto Top Up, or upgrade. Free chat and voice keep working on their own allowance.

Where to check

Settings → Usage on grok.com or in the app. It shows the percentage used by product and when it resets.

In Tokenomics

The browser extension reads grok.com's query allowance, which matches the free tier. For paid plans' weekly pool, Tokenomics' meter may not match Grok's own percentage yet; we're working on it.

Checked against each provider's own pages in November 2026. Providers change limits often, so their usage page is the final word.
