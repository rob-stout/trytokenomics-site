Help / Privacy & permissions

What can the browser extension access, and why?

Only the AI sites it works with, to read your own usage numbers. It doesn't ask for access to all websites. Here's what each permission is for.

Which sites

Only the AI sites it works with: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney, ElevenLabs, Grok, Perplexity, Leonardo, Suno and Udio. It doesn't ask for access to all websites, and it doesn't touch any others.

Why it needs them

Each site shows your usage in its own account pages, with no shared way to get it. To show it in the popup, the extension asks that site for your numbers, the same way the site's own pages do, using the sign-in you already have. That's the only reason it has access. Numbers refresh every few minutes.

What it stores

Your settings and latest numbers, in your browser's extension storage on your device. For ElevenLabs and Leonardo it also saves the access token the site gives your browser, or an API key you paste in its settings.

“Communicate with cooperating native applications”

Chrome shows this for the permission the extension uses to hand your usage numbers to the Tokenomics Mac app, if you have it installed. It stays on your computer, and your sign-in tokens are never sent over it. Without the Mac app, nothing happens.
