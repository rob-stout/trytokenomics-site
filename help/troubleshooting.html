Help / Fixing problems

Something's not working. What can I try?

Most problems clear up by checking you're signed in, clicking refresh, or reopening your browser. Here's the full checklist.

Most problems clear up with one of these:

  • Check you're signed in to that site in the same browser. Gemini, Suno, Leonardo and ElevenLabs need you to open the site in a tab at least once, because the extension reads them from the page.
  • Click refresh in the extension's popup.
  • Check the site is switched on in the extension's settings.
  • If the popup shows nothing at all, make sure you clicked Agree and turn on on the consent screen.
  • If the Mac app isn't getting readings, check that it's running and that you've opened it at least once since installing the extension. That's when it sets up the link. Then reopen the popup.
  • Just updated the extension? Quit and reopen your browser.

More specific fixes:

  • The menu bar stopped updating
  • “Couldn't load usage data” and other messages
  • macOS keeps asking for my password
  • “Authentication expired”
  • Guided setup showed an error
  • A widget shows old numbers
