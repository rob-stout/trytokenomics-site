What are Extra Usage and Credits Balance?

Extra Usage shows how much of your monthly extra-usage spending limit you've used on Claude. Credits Balance shows prepaid credits on your OpenAI account.

Extra Usage appears on the Claude tab when your plan has extra usage turned on. It shows how much you've spent of your monthly extra-usage limit, like “$12.40 / $50.00”. Extra usage is what Anthropic bills once you go past your plan's included limits.

Credits Balance appears on the Codex CLI section when your OpenAI account has prepaid credits, showing the remaining balance.

Both only appear when the service reports them. If you don't see them, your account doesn't have them.
