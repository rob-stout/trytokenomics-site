Help / Getting set up

Does the extension work without the Mac app?

Yes. It works on its own in your browser. The Mac app adds menu bar rings, widgets and coding tools, and the two stay in sync.

Yes. The extension works on its own in your browser: click its toolbar icon to see a meter for each site you're signed in to.

The Mac app adds three things:

  • Menu bar rings, so you see your closest limit without opening anything.
  • Desktop widgets in small, medium and large, plus extra large on macOS 27. See setting up widgets.
  • Coding tools the browser can't see: Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot and Cursor.

If you have both, they sync: the extension's readings show in the menu bar and widgets, and the order you set for your tabs carries across. See how the two work together.
