What do the GitHub Copilot meters show?

Paid Copilot plans show your premium requests used this month. Free plans show your monthly chat and code-completion allowances.

  • Paid plans (Pro, Pro+, Business, Enterprise): one bar, Premium Requests, with how many you've used of your monthly allowance, like “75 / 300 used”, and when it resets. Chat and code completions are unlimited on these plans, so they don't need a meter. If your premium requests are unlimited, the bar says “Unlimited”.
  • Free plan: two bars, Chat and Completions, showing how much of each monthly allowance you've used.

Paid plans showed 0% or nothing in versions before 2.10.5. Update if that's what you see.

Tokenomics reads these through the GitHub CLI's sign-in. If Copilot isn't connected yet, see the setup guide.
