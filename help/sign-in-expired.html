Help / Fixing problems

It says “authentication expired” or “Session expired”. How do I fix it?

Your coding tool's sign-in ran out. Sign in to the tool again, or click Reconnect to be walked through it.

Tokenomics uses the sign-in your coding tools keep on your Mac. When that sign-in expires, Tokenomics can't read your usage until you sign in again.

  • Easiest: click Reconnect on that tab. Guided setup walks you through signing in, and Tokenomics notices automatically when you're done.
  • Or do it yourself: sign in to the tool the usual way (for Claude Code, run claude in Terminal and sign in), then click refresh in the popover.

For web tools in the browser extension, just sign back in to the site.
