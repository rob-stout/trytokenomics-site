Help / Your numbers

How do Claude's 5-hour and 7-day limits work?

The 5-hour window is the rolling cap most people hit first; the 7-day window is the weekly ceiling. Chats and Claude Code share both.

These are Anthropic's own limits, not something Tokenomics invented.

The 5-hour window

The rolling cap most people hit first. It resets about five hours after your first message in the current block. If Tokenomics shows 80% with two hours left, you're close to a rate limit, so pace your prompts or switch tasks.

The 7-day window

The longer ceiling, resetting weekly from your first message of the cycle.

If your plan has extra usage turned on, the Claude tab also shows how much of your monthly extra-usage limit you've spent. See Extra Usage.

Why Claude has one set of limits

Claude on the web, the Claude desktop app and Claude Code all draw down the same 5-hour and 7-day windows on your subscription. One set of limits covers every surface, so Claude gets one meter with two bars. OpenAI and Google do it the other way, with a separate meter per product. See why ChatGPT and Gemini show two meters.
