How do I choose what the menu bar shows?

By default the menu bar shows whichever tool is closest to its limit. You can pin one tool instead from the popover's Menu Bar Display menu.

The menu bar icon shows one tool at a time, as two rings and a percentage.

Smart or pinned

  • Smart (most urgent) is the default. The menu bar shows whichever tool is closest to its limit, so the most pressing one is always in view.
  • Pin Tracker keeps one tool in the menu bar no matter what.

To switch, open the popover and click the display button in its footer, then choose Smart (most urgent) or a tool under Pin Tracker. The button only appears once you have two or more tools connected.

Reading the rings

  • Inner ring: the short window, the limit you're likely to hit first, like Claude's 5-hour window.
  • Outer ring: the longer window, like Claude's 7-day window. Tools with only one limit show one ring.
  • Dots: the pace dots, one on each ring.
  • Percentage: the inner ring's value.
