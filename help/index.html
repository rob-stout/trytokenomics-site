    Getting set up

    • Which AI tools does Tokenomics work with?

      From Claude Code and Cursor to ChatGPT, Midjourney and Suno. Here's the full list, what each meter tracks, and how each one connects.

    • Which browsers does the extension work in?

      Google Chrome, plus Brave, Arc, Vivaldi and Opera from the Chrome Web Store. Versions for Microsoft Edge and Safari are on the way.

    • Does the extension work without the Mac app?

      Yes. It works on its own in your browser. The Mac app adds menu bar rings, widgets and coding tools, and the two stay in sync.

    • How do the Mac app and the browser extension work together?

      The Mac app reads your coding tools, the extension reads your web tools, and they share usage numbers on your computer so each shows the full picture.

    • Does Tokenomics work on Windows or Linux?

      The browser extension does, in Chrome. The menu bar app, widgets and coding-tool tracking need a Mac for now.

    • Is Tokenomics free?

      Yes. The Mac app and the browser extension are free, with no subscription, no ads and no account to make.

    • Does the extension start reading as soon as I install it?

      No. It shows a short consent screen first, and nothing is read until you click Agree and turn on.

    • How does signing in work?

      There's nothing to sign in to. The Mac app reads the sign-in your coding tools already keep, the extension uses the sites you're signed in to, and a few tools take an API key.

    • How do I set up ElevenLabs?

      Sign in at elevenlabs.io with the extension installed, or paste an API key into the extension or the Mac app. Here's how each works and how to make a safe key.

    • How do I uninstall Tokenomics?

      Quit the app and drag it from Applications to the Trash. To remove the extension, right-click its toolbar icon and choose Remove.

    • Something's not working. What can I try?

      Most problems clear up by checking you're signed in, clicking refresh, or reopening your browser. Here's the full checklist.

    Reading your numbers

    • How do Claude's 5-hour and 7-day limits work?

      The 5-hour window is the rolling cap most people hit first; the 7-day window is the weekly ceiling. Chats and Claude Code share both.

    • Why do ChatGPT and Gemini show two meters?

      ChatGPT and Codex CLI share an OpenAI login but have separate limits, and so do the Gemini app and Gemini CLI. Tokenomics shows each limit on its own.

    • Are the numbers exact?

      Usually. Most tools report your usage directly. When one doesn't, Tokenomics estimates and labels the reading “estimated”.

    • What are Midjourney's Fast Hours?

      Fast Hours are the GPU time Midjourney gives your plan each month for Fast mode. Tokenomics shows how many you've used. Relax mode doesn't count.

    Privacy & permissions

    • Is my data sent anywhere? Does Tokenomics see my chats?

      No. Nothing goes to any server the author controls: no analytics, telemetry or tracking. The extension reads usage numbers, never your chats or prompts.

    • What can the browser extension access, and why?

      Only the AI sites it works with, to read your own usage numbers. It doesn't ask for access to all websites. Here's what each permission is for.

    • Is Tokenomics affiliated with these AI services?

      No. Tokenomics is independent and unofficial. It reads your own usage with your own sign-in.
