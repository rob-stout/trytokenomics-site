Help / Fixing problems

What does “Couldn't load usage data — check back in a few minutes” mean?

The service asked Tokenomics to slow down. It keeps showing your last numbers and tries again after a short wait. Here's that and the other error messages.

That message means the service is rate-limiting Tokenomics: it's been asked to check less often for a while. Tokenomics keeps showing the last numbers it got, waits, and tries again. Each retry waits longer: 5 minutes, then 10, 20, 40, up to an hour, unless the service says exactly when to come back.

You don't need to do anything. Hover over the “Updated…” line at the bottom of the popover to see “Rate limited — showing most recent available data.”

Other messages

  • No network connection: your Mac is offline. It retries when you're back.
  • Server error (503) or another number: the service had a problem. It usually clears on its own.
  • Couldn't read usage data: the service sent something Tokenomics didn't recognize, often after the service changed. Check for an update, and tell us if it continues.
  • Not signed in or Session expired: see signing back in.
  • Keychain access needed: see the Keychain prompt.
