Help / Fixing problems

Why does my widget show old numbers or “Open Tokenomics to sync”?

Widgets show what the Mac app last read. If the app isn't running, they can't update. Tap the widget's refresh button to open the app and refresh.

Widgets don't fetch anything themselves. They show the numbers the Mac app last read.

  • “Open Tokenomics to sync” means the widget has nothing yet. Open Tokenomics once and it fills in.
  • Old numbers usually mean the app isn't running, for example after a restart. Turn on Launch at Login so it's always there.
  • The refresh button on a widget asks the app to refresh, and opens it in the background if it's quit.

Web tools also depend on the browser extension; with your browser closed, they show the last reading, marked with its age. See how the Mac app and extension work together.
