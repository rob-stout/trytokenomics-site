Help / Getting set up

How do I uninstall Tokenomics?

Quit the app and drag it from Applications to the Trash. To remove the extension, right-click its toolbar icon and choose Remove.

The Mac app: quit it, then drag Tokenomics from your Applications folder to the Trash. What's left behind is small: your settings and the usage numbers the widgets read. For a completely clean removal you can delete Tokenomics' entry from ~/Library/Preferences, but it's harmless to leave.

Installed with Homebrew? Run brew uninstall --cask tokenomics instead.

The browser extension: right-click its toolbar icon and choose Remove. That deletes everything it saved.
